How to Join
To participate, athletes must be registered through the JAAF START system:

✔ Create an account in JAAF START
✔ Find Next Gen Athletics Club (Club ID: 518)
✔ Submit a membership request

Once approved, your child will be officially registered as part of our club and eligible to compete.
